How it works

What it does.

Your routine, in order

Add what you own and Regimen sorts it the way it should go on: cleanser, treatments, moisturizer, sunscreen. Morning and evening stay separate, and anything on a schedule only appears on the days it's due.

Clashes, caught early

Pure vitamin C needs a low pH that niacinamide raises. Benzoyl peroxide deactivates a retinoid layered over it. Regimen flags these the moment both products land in one routine, and says why in a sentence you can act on.

A scan that stays on your phone

Photograph your face and the analysis runs on-device with Core ML, giving a 0 to 100 score and a read on each zone. The photo is never uploaded for scoring, never sent to us, and never used to train anything.

Proof it's working, or isn't

The trend chart marks the day you started each product, so a change in your skin lines up against a change in your routine instead of a vague feeling that things are better.

Reorder before you run out

It learns how fast you get through a bottle from your own check-offs and predicts the day it runs dry, so you replace it in time instead of a week late.

Privacy

The scan never leaves your iPhone.

Skin analysis runs entirely on-device through Core ML. Your photo isn't uploaded to be scored, isn't sent to us, and isn't used to train a model. Only the resulting number and zone summary sync, so your history follows you to a new phone. Export everything as JSON whenever you like, and delete your account from inside the app.
